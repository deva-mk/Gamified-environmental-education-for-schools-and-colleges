# Gamified Environmental Education for Schools and Colleges

## Overview

Gamified Environmental Education for Schools and Colleges is an interactive learning platform designed to promote environmental awareness through engaging game-based learning experiences. The platform combines educational content with gamification elements such as quizzes, challenges, points, badges, rewards, levels, and leaderboards to encourage active participation and knowledge retention among students.

The system aims to make environmental education more enjoyable and effective by motivating students to learn about sustainability, climate change, biodiversity conservation, waste management, renewable energy, and eco-friendly practices.

## Features

* User Registration and Login
* Student and Administrator Modules
* Interactive Environmental Learning Content
* Quiz-Based Assessments
* Environmental Challenges and Missions
* Points and Reward System
* Badge and Achievement Tracking
* Leaderboard Ranking System
* Progress Monitoring Dashboard
* Environmental Awareness Campaign Activities
* Performance Reports and Analytics

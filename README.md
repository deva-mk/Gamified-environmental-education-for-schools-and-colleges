# EcoChronicles: Gamified Environmental Education for Schools and Colleges

##Project Overview

EcoChronicles is a gamified environmental education platform designed for school and college students. The platform transforms traditional environmental learning into an engaging and interactive experience through quizzes, challenges, badges, leaderboards, rewards, and achievement systems.

The project aims to increase environmental awareness, improve student participation, and encourage sustainable practices through game-based learning methodologies. By combining environmental education with gamification concepts, students can learn about climate change, biodiversity conservation, renewable energy, pollution control, waste management, and sustainable living in a fun and interactive manner.
